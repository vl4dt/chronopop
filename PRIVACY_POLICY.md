# Chronopop Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 22, 2026

## Introduction

Chronopop ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("the App").

## Information We Do Not Collect

Chronopop operates entirely on your device. **We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.**

### Data Storage
- All your alarms, timers, stopwatches, reminders, and app settings are stored **locally on your device** using your device's built-in storage (AsyncStorage).
- We do not have access to your data.
- Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

## Information Stored Locally

The following information is stored only on your device:

- **Alarms and timers**: Your configured time settings and preferences
- **Stopwatch data**: Lap times and running sessions
- **Reminders**: Task lists and completion status
- **App preferences**: Theme settings, haptic feedback preferences
- **Usage statistics**: Focus time tracking (stored locally only)
- **Trial status**: Information about your 30-day trial period

## Permissions

The App requests the following permissions solely for functionality:

- **Notifications**: To deliver alarm and timer notifications
- **Vibration**: For haptic feedback during interactions
- **Exact Alarms**: For precise alarm timing
- **Boot Completion**: To restore alarms after device restart

## Third-Party Services

Currently, the App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services. Your data stays on your device.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, security is handled by your device's operating system security features. We recommend keeping your device secure with a PIN, pattern, or biometric lock.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information to us, please contact us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

- Email: support@chronopop.app

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store your personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete on our servers. You maintain full control over your data stored on your device through the App's settings.
